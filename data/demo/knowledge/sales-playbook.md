# Sales Playbook

## Response time

Every inbound lead receives a first reply within one business day. Hot leads are offered a meeting in the first reply.

## Meeting length

Introductory meetings are 30 minutes long. Meetings are offered on business days only.

## What not to promise

First replies do not quote prices or delivery dates. Pricing is discussed in the meeting.

## Outbound email

Any email sent to a person outside the organisation is reviewed by a person before it is sent, unless the customer already has an open conversation with us.
