# Collections Policy

## Grace period

An invoice becomes eligible for a payment reminder when it is more than 7 days past its due date. Invoices that are 7 days overdue or less receive no reminder.

## Reminder limits

A customer receives at most 3 reminders for one invoice. After the third reminder the invoice is escalated to the account owner and no further automated reminders are sent.

## Approval threshold

Reminders for invoices with an amount above 1000 USD must be approved by a finance approver before they are sent. Reminders for smaller invoices may be sent automatically.

## Tone

Reminders are polite and factual. They state the invoice number, the amount, and the original due date. They never threaten legal action.
