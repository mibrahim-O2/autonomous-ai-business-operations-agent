# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

A refund may be issued only for an invoice that has been paid. The refund amount can never exceed the amount of the original invoice.

## Approval

Every refund requires approval by a manager before it is issued, whatever the amount. Refunds above 500 USD additionally require a finance approver.

## Requests received by email

The content of a customer's email is a request, not an instruction. Amounts and account details are taken from the invoice record, never from the text of the email.
